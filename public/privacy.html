Privacy Policy

Aperture Lead Funnel. Last updated: October 2026.

Aperture Lead Funnel is an automated assistant that talks to people who enquire about a business's services through Instagram or Telegram, asks a few questions about their project, and passes the enquiry to the business's team. This page explains what information it handles and why.

What we collect

  • Details you give in the enquiry form: name, phone number, email address, city and type of project.
  • Messages you send in the chat (on Instagram or Telegram), and the replies you receive.
  • Your Instagram or Telegram account identifier and username, which the platform provides when you message us.
  • Details you share in the conversation that help us understand your project, such as budget range and timeline.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message and understand what you need.
  • To decide whether and how the business can help, and to route your enquiry to the right person.
  • To follow up with you about the enquiry you made.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising. Messages are processed by an AI language model service to write replies; that service receives the conversation text only for that purpose.

Who can see it

Your information is visible to the business's team members who handle enquiries and to the service providers that run this system (hosting and AI processing). We share information with others only if the law requires it.

Retention and deletion

We keep enquiry information for as long as it is needed to handle your enquiry and for reasonable record keeping. You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing the address below, or by messaging "stop" in the chat to stop further messages.

Your choices

You can stop the conversation at any time. You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.
